**What needs to happen before Session 2**

These are the items that will make your conversations stronger and remove the friction in due diligence. Some of these require Toby or the legal team.

1. **Traffic data sheet** — one document showing real footfall at Nirvana (700+ people/day). Does not need to be a report. A chart or a simple factual summary is enough. This is one of your strongest proof points and it does not exist as a shareable document yet.

2. **Extension answer** — a clean two to three sentence response to "what happens with the lease extension?" that anyone on the team can deliver confidently on a call.

3. **PBG/SLF status** — a short factual statement covering where you are in the permit process and what it means (or does not mean) for buyers. Legal to assist.

4. **Exit plan** — what are the buyer's options if they need to sell before the lease ends? Secondary market, transfer process, any restrictions? Even a simple explanation removes a fear that is currently unanswered.

5. **Bankruptcy question** — what is the legal structure that protects buyers if something happens to the company? This needs a one-paragraph answer from legal.
