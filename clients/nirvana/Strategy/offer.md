## 9. Guarantee

**Two-layer guarantee:**

**Layer 1 — Return guarantee:**
Minimum 10% ROI per year for the first 2 years of hotel operations, contractually committed in the management agreement. Monthly disbursements based on actual hotel performance. If performance falls short of 10% in any given year, property management covers the difference. No exceptions. In writing.

**Layer 2 — Reservation guarantee:**
$10,000 refundable deposit to reserve your unit. Fully refundable within 14 days, no questions asked. You lock in today's price and today's unit without being committed until you've completed due diligence.

**How to deliver in a closing conversation (Susi's version — use this):**
"You receive your return every month based on hotel performance. If we don't hit 10% by end of year, property management covers the shortfall. That's in the contract."

**Key coaching note:** Never describe the guarantee as a projection or estimate. It is a contractual obligation. The language is "written into the management agreement" — not "we expect" or "we're targeting."

## 10. Pricing Anchor

**The comparison that matters is not price per sqm — it is yield on investment.**

| Anchor | Value |
|---|---|
| Total offer stack value | $1,019,300+ |
| Investment required | $179,000–$259,000 |
| Guaranteed annual income | $17,900–$25,900 |
| Realistic annual income | ~$26,850–$38,850 (15% projection) |
| Monthly income (floor) | $1,491–$2,158 |
| Lifetime club membership included | $134,000 standalone |
| 28 nights personal use/year | $151,000 over lease |

**The pricing anchor conversation:**
"You're not buying 40 sqm. You're buying $17,900 per year minimum, a lifetime membership to the club, and 28 nights a year in Bali — all managed for you, from wherever you are in the world. The suite is how you access all of that."

**Two ROI claims — stack them in this order:**
1. "The contractual floor is 10% — that's in writing."
2. "Based on our conservative occupancy model and club demand, most investors will see closer to 15%."

Never lead with 15%. Lead with 10% (certainty), then open up to 15% (upside). Reversing the order erodes trust.
