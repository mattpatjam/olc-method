## ROI and Investment Case

**Two separate ROI claims — both correct, different things:**
- **10% guaranteed** = contractual commitment (minimum floor, written into the management agreement)
- **15% annually** = projection based on conservative occupancy assumptions + club demand

Use the 10% in closing conversations ("this is what's contractually guaranteed"). Use 15% as the realistic upside ("based on conservative projections, most investors see closer to 15% annually"). Stack them: floor + realistic expectation.

## Legal and Purchase Process

**Ownership:** 27-year leasehold with option to extend
**Certifications:** PBG (Building Permit) confirmed · Full environmental, fire, and wellness certification targeting GBCI standards
**Due diligence:** Legal DD coordinated by developer, executed by licensed notary. All third-party fees borne by purchaser.

**Purchase process:**
1. Book unit — $10K refundable deposit (T&Cs apply), fully refundable within 14 days
2. Contract finalization — MOU and Management Agreement signed and notarized
3. Down payment — 40% after contract notarization (booking fee deducted)
4. Lease execution — Lease agreement executed and notarized
5. Handover — Unit delivered, purchase complete
