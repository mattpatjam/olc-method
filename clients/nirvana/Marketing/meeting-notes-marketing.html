Internal use only  ·  Do not share
Presenter Notes
60-Second Intro

"I've been working with Susi and Natasha for the past six weeks — an audit of the pipeline, then two sessions to build the offer stack and objection framework. I found three distinct buyer profiles, each with a different reason to buy and a different blocker. I wanted to share that work so we're all describing the same opportunity in the same language — and see if there's a warm channel we can run alongside the paid campaigns."

Tone: peer in the room. Not consulting. Not pitching.

Meeting Flow
  • 1 Introduce the brief — hand it over, walk page 1 quickly. Earns credibility first. 5 min
  • 2 Walk the 3 buyer profiles — p.4 of brief. Ask: which profile are ads targeting? Who's converting? 10 min
  • 3 Show the gap — pp.2–3. Flag the broken [X]% on the website explicitly. 5 min
  • 4 Propose Channel B — p.6. Their campaigns untouched. Channel B is additive. 5 min
  • 5 Make the two asks — see right column. 5 min
Do Not
  • ✕Mention any fee for the marketing work — commission is the payment
  • ✕Position OLC as competing with or replacing their team
  • ✕Get drawn into ad budgets, platforms, or campaign mechanics
  • ✕Name Susi or Natasha individually — say "the team"
  • ✕Over-promise on email outreach — "one test sequence, see what converts"
The Asks
Ask from the marketing team
Alignment on which buyer profile to lead with in the next campaign cycle. Wrong profile = wasted budget. Right profile = fast win they can point to.
Ask from Toby
Access to the member / email list for Channel B outreach.
Frame: "Before anything goes out, you approve the message. Zero risk to the brand." If he asks about commission — point to the proposal. 5% on OLC-sourced leads. Already in writing.
If they ask what you get out of this
"If the warm channel performs, I earn a commission per closed unit from those leads. Toby and I already agreed the structure. The brief today is my way of making sure paid channels work from the same foundation — that's good for everyone."

If ads come up — Market Pyramid
"Most property ads fight for the 3% ready to buy now. The member list reaches the 7–12% before they're ready — that's the uncontested market. Channel B isn't a sales tactic. It's a structural advantage."
If they show you ad copy — Dobie Test
Doesn't work: "Luxury suites from $189K. Book a viewing."  Works: "How Bali wellness investors earn guaranteed 10% income without the offshore complexity — here's the structure." Intrigue around the HOW + specific benefit.
Immediately actionable — Identity Trigger
Take their best ad, add a niche identity word: "How European investors earn guaranteed 10% from Bali wellness real estate." Meta finds a new audience pocket. Same budget, lower CPL. Test alongside control, results in 5–7 days.
