The creative brief

Three buyers.
Three stories to tell.

Every buyer has a belief that stops them from acting. The marketing job is to collapse that belief with the right story. One ad, one Instagram caption, one email per profile. Not one generic message for all three.

Profile False belief (the blocker) The story that collapses it
Pure Investor
Yield-first. Doesn't live in Bali. Compares against ETFs and property funds.
 "Off-plan Bali is risky. No track record, no guarantee I can trust." 18 of 32 units sold before the foundation was complete. Not a projection. A market verdict. The 10% is contractual: if the hotel underperforms, property management covers the shortfall. Same structure as a guaranteed hotel lease. In writing.
Lifestyle Investor
Comes to Bali for the experience. Wants a base. Compares against villas.
 "I want my own villa. A 40sqm suite isn't what I had in mind." You're not buying 40sqm. You're buying $18,900 per year minimum, a lifetime club membership, 28 nights in Bali, and zero property management from wherever you live, all for the price of a studio villa that earns nothing while you're away and costs you to maintain.
Nirvana True Believer
Already a member. Travels to Bali for Nirvana specifically. The warmest lead available.
 "I already pay for the club. Why would I buy a unit on top of that?" The buyers who bought in 6 months ago pay the same club fees you do. The difference: their suite is paying them back. They're still members. They just own. You're paying for the community either way. One of those payments returns 10% a year.
How to use these in marketing
  • Each story is a separate ad set, email, or Instagram post. Not one message trying to speak to everyone.
  • The Nirvana True Believer story converts highest. Prioritise it for the member channel and warm outreach.
  • Lead with the story, not the features. Features come after the belief shifts.

The plan

Two channels.
One goal. Zero competition.

Paid campaigns and warm outreach work different audiences at different temperatures. They don't compete. They compound.

Channel A: Paid Advertising Marketing team + Sales team
  • Marketing team runs Meta and Google campaigns, targeting cold and warm audiences
  • Leads from campaigns go to Susi and Natasha as normal. No change to their pipeline.
  • OLC contribution: message architecture, offer stack integration into ad copy, buyer profile targeting brief (this document)
  • Goal: ads that pre-sell the belief before the lead reaches the sales team
Channel B: Warm Member Outreach OLC Method
  • Target: existing gym members, email list subscribers, event attendees. Already Nirvana believers.
  • This audience converts 3 to 5 times higher than cold traffic and requires zero ad spend
  • OLC designs and runs the outreach sequence. Every response handled personally.
  • All leads from this channel go directly to OLC for qualification and closing
  • Requires: member/email export from Toby, reviewed and approved before anything goes out
