## Audit Findings Summary (June 9)

**Top 3 issues:**
1. No discovery before pitching — no timeline, budget, or comparison questions asked before presentation starts
2. No next step confirmed at end of interactions — leads go cold after "I'll send you the details"
3. Product knowledge gaps — construction specs, AC, water filtration, warranty details not known on the spot

**Secondary issues:**
- Marketing not communicating key value props (10% guarantee, lifetime membership, in-house management, scarcity)
- Referral program (1% commission) exists but never activated
- Tower 1 remaining units have no distinct positioning
- Urgency (14 units, August target) not used as a closing tool
- Natasha's capacity as a closer is untapped

**What's working:**
- Team division of labor (Natasha qualifies, Susi closes) natural and effective
- Susi's brand storytelling and objection handling are instinctively strong
- Organic referrals and repeat buyer relationships are healthy
- Payment structure flexibility (especially upfront 10% construction interest) is a strong differentiator
