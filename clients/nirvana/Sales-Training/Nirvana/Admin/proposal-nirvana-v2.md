# Nirvana Health Club — Sales Training Proposal

Hi Toby,

Good talking last week. Based on what you shared, I put together a structured engagement to get your team from 2 closings a month to 4. Two phases: an audit to understand where things are breaking down, then 5 training sessions with the team. There's an optional strategy session in between if you want to align on direction before training starts.

Here's the breakdown.

---

PHASE 1 - SALES AUDIT
2-hour session | $350

Before anything else, I spend two hours with your team watching how they work: how leads come in, how first contact is made, how they follow up, where things go quiet. Not there to judge, there to diagnose.

Deliverable: a short written brief. What's working, what's broken, what to fix first. The rest of the engagement is built on this.

---

PHASE 2 - STRATEGY SESSION (optional)
1 session with you | $500

After the audit, I present the findings and a recommended sales strategy for your team. One meeting. You get the diagnosis and the direction in one go. If you want to go deeper, we can add sessions, but one is usually enough to align before training starts.

---

PHASE 3 - SALES TRAINING PROGRAM
5 sessions x 2 hours with your sales team | $1,800

Session 1 - Lead Approach & First Contact
How your team opens conversations with new leads. The goal of every first message is one thing: get the reply. We work on short, curiosity-driven WhatsApp openers and leave with 3-5 templates tailored to your buyer profiles.

Session 2 - Follow-Up Sequences
Most leads don't close on first contact. Most teams either follow up too hard or disappear. We build a structured sequence they can use immediately: how many touchpoints, what to say at each stage, how to re-engage cold leads.

Session 3 - Qualification Framework
How to move a conversation from casual interest to real intent (budget, timeline, who decides) without killing the momentum. The team leaves with a framework so they always know where they are and what to do next.

Session 4 - Buyer Psychology & Closing
What drives a buying decision in real estate, and how to guide a conversation toward a yes without pushing. We cover the emotional triggers, how to handle "I need to think about it", and closing techniques that work in a WhatsApp environment.

Session 5 - Review, Refinement & Playbook
We go through real conversations from the weeks in between, refine what's working, and I hand over a written playbook your team keeps. This is what makes it stick.
