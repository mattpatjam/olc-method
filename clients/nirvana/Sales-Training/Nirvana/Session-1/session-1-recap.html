The frame that changes the conversation: "You are not comparing this to other apartments. You are comparing it to anything else you could do with $189K. What else gives you a contractual 10% floor, lifetime club membership, 28 nights in a world-class wellness facility, and zero management? The numbers speak for themselves."

Urgency — what is real

Price increases 5% in July. Buying now at the current price means the same contractual yield on a lower entry point — a higher effective return on capital. This is genuine, not manufactured. Use it plainly: "Once the price goes up, you get the same guarantee on more money invested."

Risk reversal

The refined guarantee: "You receive your return monthly based on hotel performance. If we fall short of 10% by year-end, property management covers the shortfall. That is written into your contract." Monthly, specific, contractual — not a promise.

Every objection — mapped to the stage where it appears

The goal is to answer every objection before the buyer raises it. Not with pressure — with proof. When the buyer feels heard before they speak, they trust you faster.

Objection Stage Answer direction Status
"Too expensive for 40sqm" First conversation Reframe: not selling sqm. Show the full stack. The comparison is not other apartments — it is other uses of $189K. Ready
"How do I know the 10% is real?" First conversation Contractual, written into the management agreement. 18 of 32 buyers committed before the foundation was done. Traffic data sheet needed to reinforce. In progress
"What about the lease extension?" Due diligence Nirvana's own business runs on the same lease — extending is in our interest first. Negotiation handled by Nirvana. Clean prepared answer needed. Needs Toby
"What about the PBG/SLF permit?" Due diligence Application registered, standard process for Bali. 90% of rentals operating here don't have full permits yet — this is normal. One-paragraph factual response removes this. Needs Toby
"What if I need to exit early?" Negotiation They can sell the suite. Matthieu handles the resale as broker — the owner does not manage the process. Only cost is the standard broker fee. No complexity, no lock-in. Ready
"The nightly rate seems too high" Due diligence Confirmed rates: $152–255 / night. Comparable wellness hotel rates in Canggu to be documented as a benchmark. In progress
"Tower 1 doesn't have the sauna / ice bath" First conversation Stop comparing. Tower 1 is a separate offer. Pure Investor: same yield, lower price, higher ROI. Lifestyle: ground floor, direct terrace, inside the life of the club. Ready
"I can only stay 28 days — what if it's booked?" Post-meeting Owner use is prioritized in the booking system. The 20% unoccupied assumption in projections already accounts for owner use periods. Ready

The objection-handling principle (Chris Voss): Label first, validate, then pivot to proof. "It sounds like you are not sure the numbers are real." (label) "That makes sense — a lot of Bali projects make claims they can't back up." (validate) "Here is the difference: our 10% is contractual, not projected. Written into the management agreement." (proof)
