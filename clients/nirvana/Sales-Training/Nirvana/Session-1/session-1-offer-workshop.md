# Session 1 — Offer Workshop: Build the Offer They Can't Refuse

*Session date: ~June 16, 2026*
*Duration: 2 hours*
*Attendees: Susi (required), Natasha (required), Marketing team (strongly recommended), Toby (optional)*

---

## Why This Session Exists

Before teaching anyone how to sell, the team needs to agree on what they're selling — not the feature list, but the actual value. Most salespeople pitch the product. The best ones sell the outcome and remove every reason not to buy.

This session uses the Hormozi offer creation framework to map The Sanctuary's complete value, identify every objection buyers have, and build a formal response to each one. Everything produced here flows directly into Sessions 2–5.

## What This Session Produces

1. Three written Dream Outcome statements — one per buyer profile
2. A scored Value Equation showing The Sanctuary's strongest and weakest levers
3. A complete buyer journey map with every objection at each stage
4. A formal Offer Stack — a solution or proof point for every major objection
5. A marketing brief for the sequence rewrite

## Session Agenda

### Step 1 — Dream Outcome Mapping (25 min)

**The question:** What does each buyer profile REALLY want?

Not "a boutique hotel suite in Bali." The emotional, financial, and lifestyle outcome underneath that. The product is the vehicle. The dream outcome is the destination.

**Exercise (15 min):**
Each person writes one sentence per profile. Keep it in the buyer's voice — what they would say to a friend, not what the brochure says.

Three profiles:

| Profile | The vehicle | The dream outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Pure Investor | $179K–$259K Bali property | Financial security. Money working without me. Smart capital deployment I can be proud of. |
| Lifestyle Investor | Bali base + investment | A life where I split my time between home and Bali, and the property pays for itself. |
| Nirvana Member | Owning part of something I love | I'm not just a gym member. I own a piece of this. I belong here. |

**Debrief (10 min):**
Facilitator synthesizes the group's answers into 3 clean written statements. These become the opening frame for every sales conversation going forward. Every pitch, every WhatsApp message, every follow-up should be traceable back to one of these three outcomes.

**Profile coaching:**
- Susi: she naturally leads with the product. Push her to say the dream outcome first, product second. "You want passive income while you're based in Europe. This is how you do that in Bali." Then the product.
- Natasha: she'll write thoughtful answers. Use her sentences — they'll be closer to what buyers actually feel.

**Connection downstream:**
Session 2 (Discovery) — the three discovery questions are designed to identify which profile you're talking to. You can't ask the right questions if you don't know what you're listening for.

### Step 2 — Value Equation Audit (20 min)

**The framework:** Hormozi's four levers that determine how much value a buyer perceives.

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

Higher value = increase the top, decrease the bottom.

**Exercise (10 min):**
Score The Sanctuary on each lever as a group (1–10). Be honest.

| Lever | What it measures | Score | What's driving it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dream Outcome | How clearly are we naming what the buyer wants? | /10 | |
| Perceived Likelihood | How much does the buyer believe it will work? | /10 | |
| Time Delay | How fast do they start seeing the result? | /10 | |
| Effort & Sacrifice | How much work does the buyer have to do? | /10 | |

**What to expect:**
- Dream Outcome will score moderate — it exists but isn't stated consistently
- Perceived Likelihood is almost certainly the weakest — proof is strong (10% guarantee, 18/32 sold, construction ahead of schedule, Kempinski contractor) but isn't being assembled and delivered systematically
- Time Delay is actually strong — ROI starts from first day of hotel operations, not from purchase
- Effort & Sacrifice is very strong — fully managed means near zero buyer effort

**Debrief (10 min):**
Identify the 1–2 weakest levers. Those become the priority for the offer stack in Step 4.

For most buyers, Perceived Likelihood is the answer: they're not sure the guarantee is real, they haven't seen proof of similar projects succeeding, they've heard stories about Bali construction delays. The offer stack builds the proof.

### Step 3 — Buyer Journey Map + Master Objection List (35 min)

**The goal:** Map every touchpoint in the buyer's journey and surface every objection or fear they have at each stage.

**The 7 stages:**

| Stage | Touchpoint | Top objections |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Awareness | Ad, social post, gets WhatsApp | "Another Bali project pitch" / "Looks expensive" / no objection yet |
| 2. First contact | Natasha's WhatsApp or email | "Who are you?" / "Why should I engage?" / "I'm not in a rush" |
| 3. First conversation | Call or site visit with Susi | "Is the ROI real?" / "What's the track record?" / "How does the income pool work?" |
| 4. Post-meeting | Follow-up period | "I need to think about it" / "Need to check with my partner" / "Still comparing options" |
| 5. Negotiation | Pushback on price or terms | "It's too much" / "Tower 1 doesn't have everything Tower 2 has" / "What if construction delays?" |
| 6. Signing | Legal + payment | "Extension uncertainty on the lease" / "Who owns the land?" / "What if I need to sell early?" |
| 7. Post-purchase | Waiting for Q1 2027 handover | "I haven't heard anything" / "Is construction on track?" / "Did I make the right decision?" |

**Exercise (25 min):**
Each person goes through the 7 stages and adds objections from their own real conversations. The audit gave us a starting list — the goal is to find the ones we missed. Focus on Stage 3 and 4 where most deals stall.

Known objections to start from (from audit + sales conversations):
- "I need to think about it"
- "How do I know the ROI guarantee is real?"
- "What if construction is delayed?"
- "I don't know you / what's your track record?"
- "Tower 1 doesn't have a private sauna or ice bath"
- "I'm not sure about the lease extension"
- "I need to check with my partner / financial advisor"
- "What if occupancy is lower than projected?"
- "What if I need to sell before the lease ends?"
- "Why is there only 27 years from handover, not from signing?"

Facilitator writes all objections on the board. Group votes on the top 8–10 they encounter most often. Those become the priority for Step 4.

**Debrief (10 min):**
Read back the top 10. Ask: "If we had a perfect answer to every one of these — delivered naturally, before the buyer even asks — would more deals close?" The answer is always yes. That's what Step 4 builds.

### Step 4 — Offer Stack: A Solution for Every Objection (30 min)

**The principle:** For each major objection from Step 3, design a response. This can be:
- A **feature clarification** — something that already exists but isn't being communicated
- A **proof point** — evidence that removes the doubt
- A **guarantee** — risk reversal that transfers the fear from buyer to developer
- A **bonus** — something added to the offer that makes the objection irrelevant

Each solution gets a name and a perceived value. Together they form the Offer Stack — delivered after the core product, before the price.

**Starting examples (pre-fill based on what we know — group adds and refines):**

| Objection | Solution | Name | Perceived value |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Is the ROI real?" | 10% minimum guaranteed in writing, 2 years (Tower 2) or 3 years (Tower 1). 18/32 already sold — existing buyers believed it enough to commit. | ROI Guarantee | Removes all financial risk for 2–3 years |
| "Construction delays?" | Monthly construction update video sent to all buyers. Top floor already done, 5 weeks ahead of schedule. | Construction Transparency Program | Full visibility during the 14-month build |
| "Track record concern?" | PT Global Development contractor — same company that built for Kempinski. Structure exists. | Developer Credibility Folder | Independent proof of construction quality |
| "Tower 1 is lesser?" | Pure Investor: same income pool return at lower price = higher ROI on capital deployed. Club Floor: ground level, direct terrace access, 30 steps from everything. | Pure Investor Positioning / Club Floor Positioning | Reframes Tower 1 as a distinct offer, not a discount |
| "Lease extension uncertainty?" | Clean prepared answer + proactive communication: "Let me tell you exactly where we are on extension before you ask." | Extension Transparency | Eliminates a fear most buyers don't voice but all have |
| "Partner / advisor needs to approve?" | Offer to get on a call with the partner or advisor directly. Have a one-page factsheet ready to send. | Partner Briefing Pack | Removes a blocker instead of waiting on it |
| "What if I need to sell early?" | Clarify the secondary market / options. If there's a buyback or transfer process, explain it. | Exit Flexibility Explanation | Removes the trapped feeling |
| "Post-purchase silence?" | Monthly update email or message to all buyers during construction. Keeps them warm and confident in their decision. | Owner Update Program | Prevents buyer's remorse during the 14-month wait |

**Exercise (20 min):**
Group refines this list. Add what's missing. Remove what's not real. Give each solution honest language — not marketing copy, but what you'd actually say on a call.

**Debrief (10 min):**
Read the full stack out loud as if you're delivering it in a sales conversation. It should feel like removing objections before they're raised, not addressing them after. If it feels like pressure, rewrite it. If it feels natural, it's ready.

This list is handed to Toby as the marketing brief — the marketing team can use it to fix the follow-up sequences.

---

### Step 5 — Risk Reversal + Urgency (10 min)

**Risk reversal:** The 10% minimum guaranteed return is already strong. Two enhancements worth discussing:
- 3-year guarantee for Tower 1 instead of 2 (needs Toby sign-off — likely already agreed in the strategy session)
- Construction completion guarantee: if handover slips beyond a defined date, developer covers the income shortfall. This requires Toby's sign-off and legal input — raise it as a question, not a commitment.

**Urgency reframe:** The facts are strong — 14 units, August target, no future phase. The problem is delivery. Susi either doesn't use urgency or uses it as a threat.

Exercise (5 min): everyone writes one sentence that states the urgency honestly without pressure. Compare. The best version goes into the closing scripts for Session 3.

Example: "I want to be upfront — there are 14 units left and we're targeting August. Once those are gone the development is closed permanently. If the timing works for you, now is genuinely the moment."
