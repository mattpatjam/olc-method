Five Sessions, One System
A quick look back before we start today's work

This is a refresher, not a test. Today isn't about learning anything new, it's about proving the system works on real conversations, and handing it over for good.

Session 1 — What We're Selling

Three buyer types, one thing each really wants: Pure Investor (money working without me), Lifestyle Investor (a life split between home and Bali that pays for itself), Nirvana Member (I own a piece of this). One-sentence offer: "Guaranteed minimum 10% ROI without the nightmare of daily management, even if you've never invested in Bali before." Buyers hesitate on "is this real?", not price. Proof beats persuasion.

Session 2 — Ask Before You Pitch

First contact earns a reply, it doesn't pitch. Discovery before presentation. Every buyer has a stated reason and a real one, the real one is usually status. You are the guide, the buyer is the hero, your job is to walk them to their own decision, not push them to yours.

Session 3 — Closing the Conversation

Tell a story before the numbers. When they hesitate, find out what's really behind it, then match a story, don't argue with data. Whatever the objection, bring it back to the Session 1 value stack. No conversation ends without a date, a time, and a reason.

Session 4 — Follow-Up, The Full Call & Tower 1

Follow-up earns a reply through Give → Tease → Ask, never "just checking in." Every call runs on FRAME: Frame, Read, Admit, Match, Engage, opened with a permission question and closed with a committed next step. Tower 1 is a different product for a different buyer, not a discount version of Tower 2.

Today, Session 5, is where all four of these get tested on real conversations, not practiced on hypothetical ones.
