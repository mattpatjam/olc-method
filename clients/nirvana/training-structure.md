# Nirvana Sales Training — Full Structure

## What We Know (Nirvana Life — The Sanctuary)

- **Team:** 2 salespeople + 1 sales admin
- **CRM:** HubSpot, leads tracked via Deals pipeline
- **Lead sources:** Mix, mostly ads — but 95% of leads are existing Nirvana Life club members (warm leads)
- **Price range:** $189K–$259K USD (Tower 1 wellness studios to Tower 2 D-type pool suites)
- **Key implication:** These are believers, not cold prospects. The sales problem is converting warm interest into a decision — expect investment objections, follow-up failures, and lack of urgency as the main gaps.

## Before You Start

**DISC results — summary**

Both completed the assessment. Results:

- **Susi H (Head of Sales) — DI: 53D / 27I / 14S / 6C**
  Natural closer. Fast, direct, persuasive, high-energy. Gets bored with nurturing and repetitive follow-up. Likely rushes past qualification to get to the pitch. Won't follow up unless forced. Will respond to competition and results framing.

- **Natasha (Salesperson) — SC: 35S / 27C / 25I / 13D**
  Natural nurturer. Patient, systematic, relationship-driven, detail-oriented. Avoids confrontation — won't close because it feels like pressure. Follows up well but can't convert. Needs structure and scripts to close; improvising freezes her.

**Team dynamic:** Near-opposite profiles. Closing AND mid-funnel nurture are both broken for different reasons. This is not a motivation problem — it's a wiring problem. Training needs to work with each profile, not against it.

**Training approach:**
- For Susi: appeal to her competitive drive, keep everything punchy, frame follow-up as money left on the table
- For Natasha: give her scripts and frameworks she can follow exactly, frame closing as helping the client decide (not pressure), use lots of roleplay before live application

## PHASE 3 — SALES TRAINING PROGRAM

**Context: 14 units remaining, August sell-out target. 5 sessions, 2 hours each (same total commitment). Session 1 added after audit — builds the offer foundation before any selling skills are taught. Sessions 2 and 3 run as two 1-hour individual sessions on the same day (Susi and Natasha have near-opposite DISC profiles — they benefit from separate coaching for discovery and closing). ### General rules for every session
- Start with a review of the previous week: what did they try, what worked, what didn't
- Teach one clear concept per session, not five
- Always leave with something concrete: a template, a script, a framework they use the next day
- End every session by assigning a task to practice before the next one
- Natasha development thread: in every session, assign Natasha one step closer to running an intro call end-to-end

### Session 1 — Offer Workshop: Build the Offer They Can't Refuse

**Added after audit. Before teaching the team HOW to sell, build a shared understanding of WHAT they're selling and WHY it's worth buying.**

**Objective:** Using the Hormozi offer creation framework, map The Sanctuary's complete value, identify every objection buyers have, and build a formal response to each one. The output of this session feeds directly into Sessions 2–5.

**Full session plan:** See `session-1-offer-workshop.md`

**Attendees:** Susi + Natasha (required). Marketing team (strongly recommended — they need the outputs for the follow-up sequence rewrite). Toby (optional — useful if he can sign off on guarantee enhancements in real time).

**Session agenda (2 hours):**

- Step 1 — Dream Outcome Mapping (25 min): Define what each buyer profile REALLY wants. Three profiles: Pure Investor, Lifestyle Investor, Nirvana Member. Output: 3 written Dream Outcome statements.
- Step 2 — Value Equation Audit (20 min): Score The Sanctuary's 4 levers (Dream Outcome, Perceived Likelihood, Time Delay, Effort & Sacrifice). Identify the weakest — almost always Perceived Likelihood.
- Step 3 — Buyer Journey Map + Master Objection List (35 min): Map every touchpoint from awareness to post-purchase. Surface every objection at each stage. Output: master list of top 10 objections.
- Step 4 — Offer Stack (30 min): For each major objection, build a solution (feature clarification, proof point, guarantee, or bonus). Give each a name and perceived value. Output: The Sanctuary Offer Stack.
- Step 5 — Risk Reversal + Urgency Reframe (10 min): Review the current guarantee and urgency language. Refine.

**Deliverables:** Dream Outcome statements (3). Scored Value Equation. Master objection list (10+). Offer Stack document. Marketing brief (hand to Toby for the sequence rewrite).

**Natasha development:** Assign Natasha to study the Offer Stack and be ready to reference it in her first intro call before Session 2.

### Session 2 — Discovery Framework & First Contact

**Now Session 2. The Dream Outcome statements from Session 1 become the lens for the discovery questions — you can't ask the right questions if you don't know what you're listening for.**

**Objective:** Get both salespeople opening conversations and gathering the information they need to close — but with different applications: Natasha uses it in HubSpot messages, Susi uses it on the phone.

**Format: Two 1-hour individual sessions on the same day.** Opposite DISC profiles require different coaching. Natasha doesn't practice closing in front of Susi, and Susi doesn't slow down for Natasha's pace. Same content, tailored delivery.

**Prep:**
- Collect 3-5 examples of their current opening messages (ask both to share before the session)
- Prepare rewrites as examples
- Prepare the 3 discovery questions framework

---

**Natasha's session (1 hour):**

- Why her current openers get ignored: all pitch, zero discovery
- The discovery framework for messages: 3 questions embedded naturally in a HubSpot sequence, not a script
- The one rule: the goal of the first message is to get a reply, nothing else
- Rewrite 2 of her actual openers live — use the exact words so she can deploy immediately
- Assign: use the new framework in all new leads before Session 3. Track how many replies change compared to before.

---

**Susi's session (1 hour):**

- Why discovery before pitching closes faster — she burns time on leads she could have qualified in 2 minutes
- The 3 questions before any presentation: timeline, budget range, what else they're comparing this to
- How to embed them naturally in a first call without sounding like a form
- Role play: open a call without discovery (her current approach), then the same call with discovery — compare outcomes
- Assign: use the 3 questions in every first call before Session 3, report back on what she learned about her active leads

---

**Deliverables:** 3-5 WhatsApp/message opener templates. Written discovery question framework (3 questions, natural language versions for both message and phone contexts).

**Natasha development:** Assign Natasha to run one full intro call end-to-end before Session 3, using the new opener and discovery questions. Susi stays available as backup but does not jump in.

### Session 3 — Closing Language & The Committed Next Step

**Now Session 3. Closing scripts pull directly from the Offer Stack built in Session 1.**

**Objective:** Get both salespeople confidently asking for the decision and ending every interaction with a confirmed next step. Natasha is now being trained to close, not just qualify.

**Format: Two 1-hour individual sessions on the same day.** Susi and Natasha need fundamentally different coaching on closing — Susi needs to slow down at objections, Natasha needs to be taught that asking for the decision is helping the client, not pressuring them. They should not practice this in front of each other.

**Prep:**
- Ask each person to bring the last deal that stalled at "I need to think about it" — actual conversation
- Prepare 3-4 closing scenarios based on what you heard in the audit
- Review Natasha's first intro call (assigned in Session 2) before her session

---

**Susi's session (1 hour):**

- The committed next step: every interaction ends with a date, a time, and a reason. "I'll send the details" is not a close.
- Handling objections without steamrolling: label it, wait, let them explain. "It sounds like the extension question is what's holding you back — is that right?" Then stop talking.
- The urgency close without pressure: "14 units, August target, no future phase. I want you to have the best options before they're gone."
- Role play: 2 scenarios she brought from active leads. Full close, start to finish.
- Assign: one committed close attempt in every active conversation before Session 4.

---

**Natasha's session (1 hour):**

- Start with a review of her first intro call from Session 2 homework. What worked, what froze her.
- Frame closing as helping: "My job is to help you get to a decision, not to convince you. Asking directly is doing my job."
- The next-step formula: "I'll send you the numbers now — can we speak Thursday to go through any questions?"
- Handling "I need to think about it": don't accept it. "Totally fair — what specifically would you want to think through? Let's address it now."
- Role play: repeat the close until asking for the decision feels normal, not uncomfortable. As many reps as needed.
- Assign: attempt a committed close in every active conversation before Session 4.

---

**Deliverable:** Written closing scripts for top 3 scenarios (shared version for both). The committed next-step formula.

**Natasha development:** By the end of this session she should have practiced asking for the decision enough times that it doesn't freeze her. That is the only metric that matters here.


### Session 4 — Follow-Up Sequences + Qualification + Tower 1 Positioning

**Combined session (was Sessions 3 and 4). Both are system and template sessions — compressible now that the marketing sequences are already rewritten and the Tower 1 offer is pre-built from Session 1.**

**Objective:** Build a personal follow-up system for Susi and Natasha, add structured qualification, and lock in the Tower 1 pitch for the remaining inventory.

**Context for the combination:** The marketing follow-up sequences are already rewritten (`follow-up-sequences-rewrite.md`) and ready to hand to the team. Personal follow-up by Susi and Natasha needs 30 minutes of practice, not a full session. Qualification is faster to teach once discovery questions are in muscle memory from Session 2. Tower 1 positioning is already half-built from the Offer Stack in Session 1.

**By now the team has discovery skills and closing language — this session adds the system that connects all interactions end-to-end.**

**Objective:** Qualify faster, stop chasing unqualified leads, and give Susi a distinct pitch for the 14 remaining units.

**Prep:**
- Ask them to bring a recent lead where they couldn't tell if the person was serious
- Prepare the BANT framework adapted for real estate (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)
- Review `tower1-offer-reframe.md` — prepare the Pure Investor and Club Floor pitches

**Session agenda (2 hours):**

Part 1 - Why qualification matters (30 min)
- Time spent chasing unqualified leads = time not spent on real buyers
- The difference between showing interest and having intent
- The audit showed 212 SQL leads, 144 meetings, 74 still warm, 8 closed. That's a 4% SQL-to-close rate. Better qualification upstream = more time on the 4%, less on the 96%.

Part 2 - Build the qualification framework (45 min)
- Progressive qualification: you don't ask everything at once
- Map when to ask what: early (need, timeline) vs. later (budget, decision-maker)
- Practice moving from small talk to qualifying questions naturally
- Handle: "I don't want to say my budget" / "I need to check with my partner"

Part 3 - Tower 1 positioning (45 min)
- Two profiles: Pure Investor (capital efficiency, same ROI at lower price) and Club Floor (lifestyle, embedded in the facility)
- Practice identifying which profile each lead matches based on discovery questions
- Role play the Tower 1 pitch for each profile — not "it's the same but without the sauna"
- Practice the urgency close: "14 units remaining, August target, once these are gone the development is closed permanently"

**Deliverable:** Qualification checklist (what to know before booking a viewing). Tower 1 pitch scripts for both buyer profiles.

**Profile coaching:**
- Susi: she skips qualification and pitches fast. Frame qualifying first as closing faster — "if you know their budget and timeline before you start, you pick the right unit for them immediately." She'll buy the efficiency argument.
- Natasha: she's uncomfortable asking direct questions. Embed qualifying questions into natural conversation. Roleplay extensively. She needs reps before she'll do it live without freezing.

### Session 5 — Review, Refinement & Playbook Handover

**Now Session 5 (unchanged). Natasha closes something in this session — solo or near-solo. This is the milestone for her role expansion.**

**Objective:** Lock in what's working, fix what isn't, hand over a system they can use without you — and confirm Natasha as a second closer.

**Prep:**
- Ask each salesperson to bring 2 real conversations from the past 4 weeks (one that worked, one that didn't)
- Review notes from all 4 previous sessions
- Draft the playbook in advance, leaving gaps to fill based on what you learn in this session
- Identify one active lead Natasha can attempt to close solo or near-solo in this session

**Session agenda (2 hours):**

Part 1 - Real conversation review (45 min)
- Go through the examples they brought, together
- What worked and why? What broke down and where?
- Refine templates and frameworks based on real conversations

Part 2 - What's changed (30 min)
- Quick round: what's working now that wasn't before?
- What's still hard? What do they need more practice on?
- Check on August target: where are they, what's the gap, what do the next 4-6 weeks look like?

Part 3 - Natasha solo close (30 min)
- Natasha runs a real or role-played close end-to-end — discovery, pitch, objection, next step
- Group feedback
- This is her proof of concept moment

Part 4 - Playbook handover (15 min)
- Walk through the full written playbook together
- Short, punchy for Susi. Systematic reference doc for Natasha.
- Explain what to do when a situation isn't covered

**Deliverable:** Written sales playbook covering:
- Buyer profiles (Pure Investor + Club Floor + member lead)
- Discovery question framework
- Opening message templates (3-5)
- Follow-up sequence (5 touchpoints)
- Qualification checklist
- Closing scripts (top 3 scenarios)
- Tower 1 pitch scripts (both profiles)
- Objection-handling guide (top 5 objections)

**Profile coaching:**
- Susi: biggest risk is reverting to instinct once training ends. Her playbook section = short, punchy scripts only. Challenge her: what's her personal record for deals in a month? What would it look like with a real follow-up system? Lock her into a target.
- Natasha: she will embrace the playbook fully. Her Session 5 work is confidence, not language — she has the scripts by now. Use her wins from the past 4 weeks as proof. She needs to hear it works, not just that the scripts work.
