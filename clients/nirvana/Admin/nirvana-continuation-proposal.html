02 — What's Still Fragile

Skill without
reps fades.

Training builds the skill. It doesn't, on its own, hold it in place once the pressure of a real call hits. Natasha has the mechanics down. What's still untested is how she holds up when a real negotiation gets complicated, a hesitant buyer, a hard number, a decision that has to happen now. That only gets built with live reps on real deals, not more theory. Susi closes fast and well, but under time pressure she still front-loads information instead of pacing the call. Both are normal. Both need someone in the room every week keeping the habit live.

Marketing is doing its job well, leads are coming in. The bottleneck isn't volume.

Of the 74 leads still marked interested, only 4 are in negotiation and 1 in due diligence. That drop happens between "interested" and "closing," which is exactly the stage the training targets. It's a coaching gap, not a lead-gen gap, and right now nothing is holding that gap closed week over week.
