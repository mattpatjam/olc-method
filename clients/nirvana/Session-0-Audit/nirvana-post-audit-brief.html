Top 3 issues to fix (priority order)
Priority 1 — Highest Impact
Value of the offer not communicated before the pitch
Buyers hear features before they understand why those features matter. Three questions must come first: what is your timeline, what is your budget range, and are you looking at other real estate? That last question opens the door to contrasting Nirvana's branded, fully managed product against generic Bali investments. This is what the entire training builds toward: an offer stack that answers every objection before it gets raised.
Priority 2
No committed next step and no urgency communicated
Every call and meeting closes with "I'll send you the details." No scheduled follow-up, no confirmed date, nothing that creates a reason to act. Every interaction must end with a date, a time, and a reminder of where the project stands: 14 units left, August target, no second phase.
Priority 3
Product knowledge gaps under live pressure
The team cannot answer basic construction questions on the spot: AC system, water filtration specs, warranty scope, club access logistics, PT company details. Every "I'll check and come back to you" is friction. For a $189K–$259K investment, it erodes trust in the developer's credibility.

Susi and Natasha have no control over these messages. They are created and sent automatically by the marketing team via HubSpot. Everything on this page is a Toby conversation, not a training item. A full rewrite of both sequences is ready to hand to marketing.
Fix now
The guarantee and the projection are both real. The problem is they appear across channels without context, so a serious investor comparing messages can't tell which is which. Lead with the guarantee, follow with the projection and the demand data behind it.

What's broken
Identity confusion from message 1 (now fixed)
The first WhatsApp was signed as Susi but sent from Natasha's profile. The rewrite corrects this: Natasha's name on all messages, and when a call is ready, she introduces Susi explicitly ("my manager Susi will reach out to set up a time with you").
All pitch, zero discovery
9 messages total — not one question asked about the prospect's timeline, budget, or goals. Every message assumes the same thing: the person needs to be convinced. Works on the small percentage already sold. Fails everyone else silently.
Repeated messages and wasted touchpoints
WhatsApp messages 4 and 5 are near-identical to message 1. Zero memory of prior contact. The construction update reads: "key structural areas are actively under construction" — a specific milestone is a trust-building moment; this version is noise.
The final email surrenders
"We completely understand if now isn't the right time... reconnect anytime." No urgency, no hook, no reason to act. It hands the decision entirely to the prospect and expects them to self-motivate. They won't.
No urgency or scarcity
None of the messages mention that 14 units remain, that August is the target, or that there is no second phase. Every message reads as if the buyer can decide whenever they feel like it.
No value proposition
Messages lead with features (boutique suites, managed hotel) without establishing why this beats any other Bali investment. Nirvana's brand recognition, 750 daily pass demand, and fully managed returns are never used as a value argument.
