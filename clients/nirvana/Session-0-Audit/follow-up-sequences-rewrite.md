# Follow-Up Sequences — Rewrite

*Prepared by: Matthieu Jammers*
*For: Marketing team / Toby sign-off*
*Context: Current sequences reviewed June 9, 2026. Rewritten to align with discovery-first principles and remove credibility risks.*

---

## What Changed and Why

The current sequences broadcast. These sequences open a conversation. The shift:
- One consistent ROI figure: 10% minimum guaranteed (use this everywhere, nothing else)
- First contact establishes identity clearly: one name, one person
- Every message ends with a specific next step, not an open invitation
- Discovery through timeline, not generic lifestyle vs. investment framing
- Real demand data (750 day passes) replaces vague ROI comparisons
- Construction update becomes a genuine trust-building moment with specific milestones
- The final touchpoint uses a pattern interrupt and a file-close, not a quiet surrender

These are recommendations for the marketing team. The sales team does not control these messages.

## Notes on Implementation

**For the marketing team:**
- Use "Natasha" consistently as the sender identity. She is the first point of contact. Do not sign as "Susi" in automated sequences. Susi closes; Natasha qualifies. Keep the roles clean.
- Use the layered ROI framing consistently: "10% minimum guaranteed for the first 2 years of operations. Based on current demand, projections go up to 15% net." The guarantee anchors trust. The projection creates upside. Never present them as the same thing or use either figure alone without context.
- Never send two messages with identical or near-identical copy. If a prospect has been in the sequence for 3 weeks, they have read your first message. Treat them accordingly.
- The construction update is your strongest trust-building touchpoint. Use a specific milestone every time, not vague progress language.
- Message 5 is a deliberate pattern interrupt. It works because every previous message was polite. Do not soften it.

**For the strategy session with Toby:**
- The ROI inconsistency needs to be fixed before any other change. It is a credibility risk on every active lead right now.
- The sequence rewrite is a deliverable that can be handed to the marketing team directly. It is outside the sales training scope but a natural extension of the engagement.
