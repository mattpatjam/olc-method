# Pricing Model

*Last updated: 2026-06-16.*

---

## Core Principle

No retainer. No monthly fee. No payment unless deals close — except for the onboarding fee.

The onboarding fee is the one upfront cost. It covers discovery, offer design, and sales system setup. It also filters out developers who aren't serious.

---

## Fee Structure

### 1. Onboarding Fee (upfront, one-time)

**$15,000–$20,000 USD.** Scoped per project based on complexity and what's included.

**What it always covers:**
- Discovery session (on-site or remote, 2–4 hours)
- Grand Slam Offer design for their specific project and buyer profile
- Sales playbook (scripts, objection handling, pipeline stages)
- One revision round included

**What increases the fee:**
- Marketing and lead gen strategy brief (channel mix, ad brief, funnel design, budget guidance)
- Full sales infrastructure setup (CRM, WhatsApp sequences, lead qualification flow)
- Coordination with a content creation or media buying partner

**What OLC does not include in any tier:**
- Content creation (photography, videography, ad creatives) — referred to a specialist partner
- Website builds — referred out

---

### 2. Campaign Infrastructure (monthly, pass-through)

**~$500/month** — the tools that run the client's campaign. Passed through at cost, no markup, no management fee on top.

Covers:
- CRM and pipeline management (GoHighLevel)
- WhatsApp follow-up and automations (Wati)
- Landing pages, lead routing, reporting

Starts when the campaign goes live. Client pays this directly or as a monthly reimbursement to OLC. Full transparency — the client can see exactly what the tools cost.

**Pitch framing:** A7A charges $1,500/month management fee + tools. OLC charges $0 management fee + ~$500 in tools. That is the comparison to make.

---

### 3. Performance Fee (per unit closed)

**5% of transaction value** — when Matthieu closes the sale personally.

**2.5% of transaction value** — when Matthieu generates the lead only and the developer's team closes.

Marketing spend (~2.5% per unit) is always the developer's cost, paid directly to ad platforms. It is not included in OLC's fee.

**For bulk / family office deals:**
- Flat fee per transaction rather than a % of total
- Avoids runaway % on large portfolio buys
- Negotiate per engagement

---

### 4. Retainer (conditional — managed marketing only)

If the developer wants Matthieu to manage an outsourced marketing team on their behalf (media buyer, creative partner, etc.), a monthly retainer is added to cover those external costs. This is a pass-through, not Matthieu's margin. Scoped per engagement.

This does not apply to the base model — it only activates when Matthieu is actively managing third-party vendors for the client.

## What a Developer Actually Pays Per Unit

| Scenario | OLC fee | Marketing spend | Infrastructure | Total per unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Matthieu closes | 5% | ~2.5% | ~$500/mo | ~7.5% + infra |
| Matthieu generates lead, developer closes | 2.5% | ~2.5% | ~$500/mo | ~5% + infra |

**Plus onboarding fee ($15K–$20K, one-time, amortized across all units sold).**

## Value-Driven Pricing Pitch

Never quote the fee in isolation. Frame it against the alternatives:

| Approach | Upfront | Ongoing | Per unit closed | Developer risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A7A + Lead Estate | $19,800 setup | $1,500/month | 5% commission + 2.5% ad spend = ~7.5% | Pays setup + monthly before a single unit sells |
| Prosper Marketing (end-to-end, AU) | $10,000 onboarding | $5,000/month | ~$3,000 ad spend per unit (no commission) | $5K/month whether units close or not |
| Traditional agent | $0 | $0 | 2–5% (agent sources own leads) | Developer still runs ads, no close guarantee |
| In-house sales hire | Recruitment cost | $2,000–5,000/month salary | — | Full overhead, no close guarantee |
| **OLC Method** | **From $5,000** | **$0** | **5% on close + ~2.5% ad spend** | **Retainer only if OLC manages marketing team** |

**Cost-per-unit comparison at $150,000/unit:**
- A7A + Lead Estate: ~$11,250/unit (7.5%) + $19,800 startup + $1,500/month
- Prosper: retainer-only, no commission — low per-unit cost but $5K/month regardless
- OLC at 5%: $7,500 + $3,750 marketing = $11,250/unit — same as A7A, but from $5K onboarding, no monthly, and an actual dedicated closer
