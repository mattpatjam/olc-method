# Ideal Client Profile

---

## Primary ICP — Southeast Asia Real Estate Developer

**Who they are:**
- B2B or B2C real estate developer (they own the product they're selling)
- Residential, commercial, or mixed-use projects — the category matters less than the quality
- Active anywhere in Southeast Asia, with deep specialty in Bali and Lombok
- Has a project with real value — concept, location, design, or vision that justifies a serious buyer's attention
- OLC selects partners carefully — trust and long-term relationship are prerequisites, not afterthoughts

**What makes OLC the right fit:**
- OLC sells what small local agencies can't — value-based selling to buyers who understand ROI
- Target buyer is not looking for cheapest price per sqm — they want to understand the full investment thesis
- Fully managed, fully serviced, concept-driven projects with a narrative beyond the walls

**Why this segment:**
- They have a premium product worth selling — Matthieu designs the offer that unlocks its full value
- They typically lack a structured sales process (WhatsApp-driven, agent-dependent, no playbook)
- They can't afford a full-time VP of Sales but need one
- Performance-based fee is easy to justify — they only pay when money changes hands

---

## What Makes a Client Ready

**Green lights:**
- Project is ready to launch or already has inventory sitting unsold
- Developer has a marketing budget (willing to pay for ads directly)
- There is a real, differentiated product — lifestyle, wellness, views, location story
- Decision-maker is accessible (founder, CEO, or head of development — not a committee)
- They've tried traditional agents and are frustrated with results
- They're not already locked into a sales agency contract

**Red flags — do not pursue:**
- Commodity developer (generic product, no story, competing on price only)
- No marketing budget or unwilling to pay for ads
- Developer who wants leads, not closings ("just bring me inquiries")
- Decision-maker is not accessible or not serious about a real partnership

## Secondary ICP — Family Offices / Institutional Buyers

**Who they are:**
- Family offices or investment groups acquiring multiple units for a portfolio
- Typically European, Swiss, Australian, or Singaporean
- Buying for rental yield, capital appreciation, or lifestyle diversification
- Deal size: 3–10 units minimum, $500K–$5M total

**How they come up:**
- Not the primary outreach target — these relationships come through developer clients
- When a developer has a bulk-buy conversation, Matthieu steps in to run the close
- Different pitch: yield, management, exit, legal structure — not lifestyle

**How to pitch them:**
- Lead with numbers: ROI, yield %, comparable exits, tax structure
- Address the "how do I manage this from Europe/Australia" question immediately
- Use the Bali legal structure knowledge (PT PMA, Hak Pakai) as a credibility anchor

---

## Decision-Makers to Target

For developer clients:
1. **Founder / CEO** — final budget decision, fastest close if convinced
2. **Head of Sales** — daily pain of managing agents and tracking pipeline, strong internal champion
3. **Co-founder or development director** — operational owner, cares about project success metrics

Avoid: marketing coordinators, junior sales staff, project managers without budget authority

---

## Pain Signals (A Client Is Ready If...)

- They have units sitting unsold for 3+ months with no clear reason why
- They're posting on LinkedIn or Instagram about the project but getting no traction on sales
- They're relying entirely on referrals or walk-ins — no structured outreach
- They recently fired an agent or are disappointed with their current agency
- They're hiring a marketing or social media manager (they have budget, they know they need help)
- They're a new project launch with no sales infrastructure in place

## ICP Scoring (Quick Reference)

Score each prospect 1–3:

| Factor | 1 (weak) | 2 (ok) | 3 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product differentiation | Commodity | Some story | Strong concept/lifestyle/location angle |
| Marketing budget | None confirmed | Maybe | Confirmed / already running ads |
| Decision-maker access | Unknown | Head of sales | Founder/CEO |
| Sales infrastructure | Active agency | Ad hoc | None (needs help) |
| Geography | Outside SEA | SEA non-core | Bali or Lombok |
| Relationship potential | One-off transactional | Open to partnership | Actively seeking long-term partner |

**Score 13+:** Priority outreach
**Score 9–12:** Active pipeline
**Below 9:** Deprioritize

## Messaging by Persona

### Founder / CEO
Lead with business outcome: "Units sitting unsold cost you more than my fee. I close, you pay. Simple."
They care about speed and margin. Don't pitch the process — pitch the result.

### Head of Sales
Lead with pain: "Your team is great at relationship management. I handle the hard closes. Together we convert more."
They want to win. Frame Matthieu as the weapon, not the replacement.

### Developer with no sales team
Lead with relief: "You built a great product. I build the system that sells it and close the first wave myself."
They're overwhelmed. Make it feel turnkey.
