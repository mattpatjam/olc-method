*Completed: 2026-06-15*

## 1. Dream Outcome

A real estate developer with a premium project — 10 or more units at $200K+ per unit — ends the engagement with every unit sold, capital freed, and a clear case study to take into the next project. Not just a sales agent. A full system: offer design, marketing, lead generation, and closing, run by one partner who only gets paid when units sell.

## 2. Avatar

Senior decision-makers at boutique real estate development companies in Bali and Southeast Asia. The project is strong — construction quality is high, the concept is differentiated, the pricing is premium. What's missing is a sales system that matches the quality of what they've built. Most are working with local agents or scattered vendors who can't explain the project value and can't close.

Minimum project: 10 units at $200K+ per unit (~$2M total inventory).

## 5. Offer Stack

### Component 1: The Setup
*Funded by the onboarding fee. Delivered before any campaign goes live.*

- Project audit and honest positioning assessment
- Unique value-driven offer document (how the project is packaged and sold)
- ICP profile and buyer persona (who gets targeted and why)
- PDF property cards — one per unit type or floor plan, individually tracked by URL in CRM. Primary top-of-funnel lead asset. A unit getting high downloads with zero form submissions signals a pricing or creative problem before more budget is spent.
- 30/60/90 onboarding timeline (what happens in the first three months)

What this gives the developer: a clear picture of the project from a buyer's perspective, a sales document that actually captures the value, and a set of trackable lead assets ready before the first ad goes live.

**Standalone value: $5,000**

---

### Component 2: The Lead Machine
*Developer pays ad spend directly to platforms. OLC designs and manages the campaigns.*

- Bespoke lead gen strategy per project (channels, targeting, messaging, ad brief)
- Marketing assets brief (creative direction, copy brief, visual guidelines)
- Campaign setup and management (Meta primary, Google secondary)
- Weekly spend reports and CPL tracking
- Campaign pause clause if CPL drifts past the agreed benchmark

What this gives the developer: a lead gen system built specifically for this project and this buyer profile — not a generic ad campaign recycled from the last client.

**Standalone value: $3,500/month**

---

### Component 3: The Sales Engine
*Performance fee only. OLC earns when units close.*

- Sales playbook delivered pre-launch (after ICP and offer are locked)
- Matthieu as dedicated closer for the full sales cycle
- CRM access for the developer (real-time pipeline, every lead visible)
- Exclusive lead clause (leads stay with this project, legally protected)
- Lead qualification criteria defined and locked at onboarding
- Monthly performance recap (automated, on top of CRM access)

What this gives the developer: a closer who knows the project as well as they do, with full transparency on every lead and no incentive to cherry-pick or shortcut.

**Standalone value: 5% of deal value, performance-only**

## 6. Scarcity

OLC takes 1-2 projects per year. That's not a marketing line — it's how the model works. When Matthieu is personally running offer design, campaign management, and closing on a project, splitting attention across multiple developers and still guaranteeing the result isn't possible.

When a slot is available, it's open. When it fills, the next opening could be 6-12 months out depending on where the active project sits in its sales cycle.

---

## 7. Urgency

**Cash flow clock:** Every month a project sits unsold is dead capital for the developer. Money that can't go into the next acquisition, the next build, the next deal. The faster the project sells, the faster the developer moves on. OLC's system exists to compress that timeline.

**Slot timing:** OLC only takes 1-2 projects per year. If a slot is open and a developer hesitates, they're not "thinking about it" — they're handing the slot to whoever moves next. The next opening might be 6-12 months away.

## 8. Bonus Stack

### Bonus 1: The Proof Package
*Kills the "never heard of you" objection before it becomes a blocker.*

A documented breakdown of Matthieu's track record: 5 years at Bali Exception, approximately 50-100 villas personally closed, plus the Nirvana Health Club case study once the first engagement closes. Developer can verify competency before committing rather than betting on someone unknown.

Value: the months of trust-building it replaces.

### Bonus 2: The Campaign Accountability Dashboard
*Kills the "you'll burn through my budget" fear.*

Real-time CPL tracking, weekly spend reports, and a campaign pause clause if cost per lead exceeds the agreed benchmark. No black box. The developer sees exactly where every dollar goes and has a contractual right to pause the campaign if the numbers go off track.

Delivered via a white-labeled Looker Studio dashboard connected to Meta Ads, Google Ads, and GA4. Client gets a shareable link. No login required. Metrics visible at all times, not just at reporting time.

**Standalone value: $1,500**

---

### Bonus 3: The 90-Day Milestone Map
*Kills the "I don't know what you'll actually do" objection.*

A written week-by-week plan for the first 90 days: what launches when, what KPIs are tracked, what triggers a strategy adjustment. Delivered at the start of the engagement so there's no ambiguity about what the work actually looks like day to day.

**Standalone value: $500**

---

### Bonus 4: The Broker Question (live, not a document)
*Kills the "I already have agents handling this" objection.*

When a developer raises their existing agency or broker network, the response is a calibrated question: "What's their performance been so far — and what made you take this call?" Most of the time, the developer answers their own objection. The existing setup isn't working well enough, and they already know it.

---

### Bonus 5: The Lead Intelligence Report
*Delivered at the end of the engagement. Makes the next project stronger.*

A structured report built from AI transcripts of every sales call throughout the engagement. What questions came up most. What objections appeared. What messaging landed and what didn't. What the leads said about the project, the price, the competition, and the concept. The developer walks away with intelligence they can feed directly into their next project — positioning, pricing, product decisions, and marketing angles all informed by real buyer conversations.

Free of charge. Delivered at close of engagement.

**Standalone value: $2,000+ (market research equivalent)**

## 9. Guarantee

**The 90-Day Commitment Guarantee**

If no signed reservation or letter of intent is received within 90 days of campaign launch, the full onboarding fee is refunded.

Condition: the developer must have fulfilled their side of the engagement — ad spend paid as agreed, creatives approved on time, access provided to the project team. If the developer stalls the launch, the 90-day clock doesn't start.

This guarantee exists because OLC only takes projects it believes it can sell. If the system doesn't produce results in the first 90 days, the developer should not be holding the cost.

## 10. Pricing Anchor

**What the developer is unlocking:**

On a 10-unit project at $200K per unit ($2M total inventory):
- Developer net margin (15-30% of revenue): $300K-$600K in profit
- Every month the project sits unsold: capital tied up, next project delayed
- Standard agency commission (2-3%): $40K-$60K — no closer, no marketing system, no guarantee
- OLC commission (5%): $100K — offer design included, campaign management included, Matthieu closes personally

**The frame:** You're going to pay a commission either way. The question is whether you pay 2-3% to someone who lists your units, or 5% to a partner who builds the system, runs the campaigns, closes the deals personally, and refunds the entry fee if it doesn't perform in the first 90 days.

**Pricing:**

| Component | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding fee | $15,000-$20,000 | Scoped per project. Paid upfront. Funds The Setup. |
| Performance fee (full) | 5% of closed deal value | OLC runs offer, leads, and closing |
| Performance fee (leads only) | 2.5% of closed deal value | OLC runs offer and leads; developer's team closes |
| Minimum project | ~$2M total inventory | 10+ units at $200K+ per unit |

Pricing is discussed privately after fit is confirmed. No public rate card.

## Internal Notes

- The selection process is a feature, not a filter. "We only work with projects we've validated" signals quality to the developer.
- Never discount the fee or commission. If a developer pushes back on price, add value to the stack instead.
- The guarantee is only as strong as the conditions attached to it. Developer obligations must be clear in the contract before the 90-day clock starts.
- Offer fatigue is real. The wrapper (name, copy, marketing angle) can be refreshed every 3-6 months without changing the actual service.
- Target minimum: $2M total inventory. Anything smaller compresses the margin too much for a 6-month commitment.
- Pricing note (June 2026): at $200-300K per unit, a 10-unit project generates $100K-$150K in commission plus the onboarding fee. Two projects per year puts OLC at $230K-$340K. The 5% holds — minimum project size is the lever to protect the margin.
