# OLC Method — Strategy

*Last updated: 2026-06-14*

---

## What This Is

A performance-based outsourced sales and marketing operation for B2C real estate developers with value-driven projects. OLC builds the entire system — from unique value offer design to marketing strategy and lead generation to closing — and takes full accountability for the result.

This is built alongside Bali Exception as a transition vehicle. Goal: replace Bali Exception as the primary income source once the model validates.

**How OLC operates:**
- Integrated partnership model — OLC is embedded in the project, not a vendor
- We select who we work with and limit the number of active projects per year to guarantee full focus and attention to detail
- Three pillars: Offer (O), Leads (L), Close (C) — each built bespoke per project and market

---

## What This Is NOT

- Not a traditional real estate agent (agents list and source leads, OLC redesigns the product, builds the sales system, and closes the deals)
- Not a consulting retainer (no monthly fee regardless of results)
- Not a fractured-accountability model (no splitting responsibility between a marketing agency, a lead gen agency, and a closer — OLC owns the full cycle)
- Not carrying the developer's marketing costs (developer pays ad spend, website, and marketing assets directly — OLC directs, developer funds)

---

## Core Positioning

**"We build the offer, generate the leads, and close the deals. You pay an entry fee to get started. The rest, we earn when you sell."**

The contrast that makes this obvious:
- Prosper Marketing: $10K onboarding + $5K/month retainer before they close a single unit
- A7A + Lead Estate: $19,800 setup + $1,500/month retainer — and they still don't close
- OLC: starts at $5K, no retainer, performance fee only when units sell

---

## How an Engagement Works

1. **Onboarding** — Developer pays onboarding fee (starts at USD 5,000, varies by scope). This filters for commitment and funds the initial build phase.
2. **Offer Design** — Deep-dive with the developer to build a unique value-driven offer: what they're selling, how it's positioned, what value is stacked, what makes buyers say yes. Output: offer document, sales playbook, ICP profile.
3. **Marketing Build** — Sales materials, project website, visual identity, ad creatives. Developer pays production costs. OLC directs the brief.
4. **Lead Generation** — Bespoke lead gen strategy designed for this project's buyer profile, price point, and market. Channels, messaging, targeting, and the full lead-to-first-conversation journey mapped per project. Developer pays ad spend directly.
5. **Sales + Closing** — Matthieu runs the full sales cycle: inbound qualification, discovery calls, demos, objection handling, signed contract.
6. **Performance fee** — 5% of closed deal value. 2.5% if scope is lead generation only.
7. **Optional guarantee** — If zero closings within 60 days, Matthieu refunds the strategy fee.

## The Underlying Framework (Internal Reference)

Applying Hormozi's value offer methodology:
- **Dream outcome** — for the developer: project sold out, on time, without carrying retainer cost or managing a fragmented vendor stack
- **Value stack** — unique offer design + bespoke lead gen strategy + marketing build + full sales cycle + closing. Each component has standalone market value.
- **Risk reversal** — performance-only compensation on the back end + optional 60-day guarantee on the strategy fee
- **Irresistible framing** — "The developer pays when money actually changes hands"

*Note: externally, all Hormozi terminology is replaced with plain language. "Grand Slam Offer" → "unique value-driven offer." No references to Hormozi, Gym Launch, or $100M Offers in any client-facing material.*

## What's Working in Bali (Local Reference Points)

**Prosper Marketing (Australia-based, Gareth Colby):**
12 signed contracts with deposits in 8 weeks on a ~$32M development. 3–4 units/week being closed. Target market: Australians. They do everything end-to-end. This is the best-performing model in the market — and they're not accessible to most Bali developers.

**A7A + Lead Estate (on Oma Sora, Umalas — 15 units, $155K avg):**
Strong marketing infrastructure ($19,800 setup). But three parties, no closer, blame gets diffused. 3 months of brand work done, ads just launched, no results yet. OLC's wedge: A7A builds the system. OLC runs it AND closes.

**Lead Estate + UTAMA Bali:**
"Helped our project sell out" — confirmed that performance-driven systems work in the Bali luxury market. They don't close. That's OLC's entry point.

**What Bali buyers respond to:**
- Rental yield numbers (not vague "strong returns")
- Turnkey management bundled into the purchase
- Exit strategy transparency (most developers avoid this — it's a differentiator)
- Social proof from similar European/Australian buyers
