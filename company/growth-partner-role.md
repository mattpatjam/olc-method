## Why this role, why now

OLC's own Grand Slam Offer has been blocked on the digital marketing gap since phase 1 (see `tracker.md` Blockers). Matthieu is DI on DISC (sales-strong, low S/C), and the pattern holding OLC back isn't the sales methodology, it's marketing execution: paid ads, content, funnels, ongoing follow-through on a build that isn't a one-time task.

Decision made 2026-07-23: staged path, not an immediate equity co-founder.
- Don't hand out equity before there's a track record. OLC has one client (Nirvana) and growth is deliberately paced until Matthieu leaves Bali Exception, there's no urgency forcing a founder-level decision now.

## Deal structure

**Comp during trial: revenue share, not flat fee.** OLC itself only gets paid when a client deal closes (performance-based model, see `company/pricing.md`), so Matthieu can't front cash for flat per-lead fees without personally eating risk he's trying to avoid. Revenue share is the only cash-neutral option, and it mirrors the model OLC already sells to clients.

- **Split:** 20% of OLC's total revenue per deal (onboarding fee + performance fee combined, not just the performance slice), effective from the first attributable deal. Flat from day one, no stepped intro rate.
- **Attribution rule:** first come, first served, decided by CRM entry. Whoever logs the lead/contact first gets credit for that deal. Simple and deterministic, avoids subjective attribution debate. Depends on both parties actually logging into the CRM consistently, that discipline needs to be set as a ground rule from day one, not left implicit.
- **Trial length:** 3-6 months, gated by milestones, not the calendar: (1) funnel/content system live, (2) first qualified leads flowing, (3) first deal closed attributable to their work.
- **Equity trigger:** after 2 closed deals attributable to their marketing, or 6 months of consistent delivery (whichever comes first), have the conversation about converting to a real equity stake (standard vesting, e.g. 1-year cliff over 4 years).

**How to frame it to a candidate:** founding growth partner, not intern or unpaid gig. Be upfront about stage (1 client, bootstrapped, no salary yet), paired with three real commitments: revenue share in writing from day one, an explicit milestone path to equity (not a vague "maybe someday"), and real ownership over marketing strategy and brand, not just task execution.

**How the build actually works:** not "Matthieu builds the funnel solo, then hires someone to run it." Offer, strategy, and foundations get built together, Matthieu and this person, from day one. Once that foundation is set, they own ads execution going forward. The trust being extended is on the marketing execution itself, not on the strategic thinking behind it, that part stays collaborative.

**Ad spend is not an OLC cash question.** Per OLC's existing business model (`company/offer.md`), the developer client funds ad spend directly, OLC runs the campaigns but never fronts media budget. This removes what would otherwise be a real gap in the trial period (no ad testing budget = milestones can't be hit).

## Core responsibilities

Mapped to what OLC's methodology actually needs built (`methodology/phase-2-leads.md`):

- Own paid ads execution (Meta primary, per OLC's ad creative framework: hook sources, 3 ad types, High-Value Dobie format)
- Build and maintain the funnel: landing pages, SLO structure, webinar/presentation assets
- Write and manage the indoctrination email sequence (9-email SOAP opera spec)
- Organic content and brand consistency across OLC's channels
- Track and report campaign performance (CPL, conversion, attribution) so the revenue-share split stays clean and disputes don't happen

## Must-have skills

- Direct hands-on Meta Ads experience (not just strategy, actual account management, creative testing, budget optimization)
- Copywriting: ad copy, email sequences, landing page copy
- Comfortable working from an existing brand/offer foundation (OLC's brand-foundation.md, offer.md are already built) rather than starting from zero
- Basic funnel/landing page build capability (or a clear plan for who builds it)

## Nice-to-have

- Real estate or high-ticket B2C experience (Bali market a plus, not required)
- Organic content/social growth track record
- Comfortable with a revenue-share/earn-in structure rather than fixed salary from day one (this itself is a filter, most people who balk hard at this aren't the right fit for a founding role)
