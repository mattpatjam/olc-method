# Phase 0 — Audit | *Clarity*

*Version: 1.0 | Created: 2026-06-18*

---

## Purpose

Show the developer what their current situation is actually costing them. This is the epiphany — the moment they go from "curious about OLC" to "I need this now."

Phase 0 is free. It has no number in client presentations. It's how prospects become clients.

---

## What OLC Does

- Reviews inventory, pricing, and existing pipeline data
- Assesses current team structure and sales activity
- Evaluates existing messaging and conversion rates
- Calculates the cost of inaction: units unsold × price × time on market

---

## What the Client Provides

- Access to relevant team members for a 60–90 min conversation
- Existing marketing materials, pricing sheet, pipeline data (if any)
- Honest answers about how they're currently selling

---

## Audit Brief — Output Template

Deliver a 1-page brief (HTML or PDF) within 1–3 days of the initial conversation.

### Structure

**What's Working**
- List any genuine strengths: location, design quality, existing relationships, prior sales, anything real

**What's Broken**
- The specific gaps — messaging, lead source, qualification, follow-up, offer design, team structure
- Be direct. Name the problem, not the symptom.

**Opportunity Assessment**
- Cost of inaction: units unsold × unit price × months sitting = the number they're leaving on the table
- Projected upside with OLC: conservative estimate based on inventory, price point, and timeline
- Recommended next step

---

## Delivery Notes

- Keep it to one page. The goal is clarity, not a report.
- Lead with the cost of inaction — the number lands harder than any feature list.
- The post-audit brief (client-facing) is a different document than the internal audit. Don't conflate them.
- The brief ends with a clear recommended next step, not an open question.

**Duration:** 1–3 days
**Cost:** Free
