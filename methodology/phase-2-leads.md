## Market Research Foundation (Do Before Any Creative Work)

**Spend 80% of Phase 2 prep time on this. Nothing else comes close in ROI.**

The biggest mistake in running developer ads: writing copy from the developer's perspective ("unique architecture," "premium lifestyle," "excellent ROI"). Buyers don't buy developer language. They buy their own words reflected back at them.

### How to build the market brief for each client

Before writing a single headline or brief for a designer:

1. **Mine the buyer intake forms** — every discovery call should have asked: "What made you start looking?" / "What's held you back?" / "What else have you considered?" Copy their exact phrases. These become headlines.
2. **Study comparable project reviews** — Facebook, Google Reviews, property forums. What language do buyers of similar projects use when they describe what they wanted? What language do they use for hesitation?
3. **Map the buyer against the Market Pyramid:**

```
    [3%]   Ready to buy NOW      → Someone already searching with intent (Google Ads)
   [7%]    Gathering info        → Curious, comparing projects (Meta ads + proof-first content)
 [12%]     Problem-aware         → Knows they want a Bali investment, not searching yet
[60%+]     Problem-unaware       → Has the money, hasn't considered real estate in Indonesia
```

Most developer campaigns fight for the 3%. OLC's edge: build for the 7% and 12% too — through education, proof, and LIVO — and you also capture the 3% automatically.

4. **Identify the Dream Buyer (20/80 rule)** — the top 20% of a developer's buyers will generate 80% of the revenue. For Nirvana: probably the Lifestyle Investor (high connection to Bali, can afford, multiple visits per year). Build the ad creative, the landing page, and the offer framing around that one person. The others still convert; they're just not the primary target.

5. **Design the LIVO (Low Investment Value Offer)** — a free piece of value that:
   - Answers the Dream Buyer's most pressing question without a sales call
   - Captures leads from all pyramid levels (not just ready-to-buy)
   - Establishes OLC / the developer as the educator and authority

Examples for real estate: "The Bali Buyer's Guide: How to Structure a Villa Investment for Maximum Yield and Exit Protection" / "The Nirvana Investment Breakdown: Unit-by-Unit Income Model" / "7 Questions to Ask Before Signing Any Bali Lease Agreement"

**Never run ads to a "Book a Call" offer alone.** That only captures the 3%. The LIVO captures all layers.

**Meta Ad Creative Framework (Sabri Suby — confirmed x4)**

Every ad must pass the High-Value Dobie test before launch:

```
[Blind Clickbait]  ←——————————→  [Naked Benefit]
  Gets everyone, converts no one    Right audience, no reason to click
                       ↓
               HIGH-VALUE DOBIE
         Intrigue around the HOW + Specific targeted benefit
                   → This wins
```

Example for Nirvana: "European investors are using this one structure to earn guaranteed income from Bali wellness real estate — here's how it works."
- Intrigue: "this one structure" (HOW is hidden, creates curiosity)
- Benefit: "guaranteed income from Bali wellness real estate" (specific and qualified)
- Not: "Nirvana Sanctuary — luxury suites from $195K" (naked benefit, no reason to click)

**The identity trigger hack (for audience expansion):**
Take a winning ad and add a niche keyword to the headline. Meta's algorithm targets by analyzing creative — the keyword signals which audience pocket to find.
- Generic: "How to earn guaranteed 10% annual yield from Bali real estate"
- Niched: "How European investors earn guaranteed 10% annual yield from Bali real estate"
- Niched: "How Singapore-based professionals earn guaranteed 10% annual yield from Bali real estate"

Same ad, different identity signal = different audience unlocked, lower CPL.

**Four creative formats for real estate (in priority order):**

1. **Native** — iPhone-style photo that looks like a real guest or owner's post. Does not look like an ad. Most reliable. Pair with the property, an owner moment, or a Nirvana experience — no stock photography.
2. **Breaking News** — "Bali wellness resort investors are selling out this development in weeks — here's what's driving it." Evergreen format. Works in every market.
3. **SMS Screenshot** — simulated WhatsApp conversation about the investment. "I just got the yield report from Nirvana, you have to see these numbers." Feels native to how real people actually talk about property purchases.
4. **Native Social Post** — dual-image format (primary image + secondary smaller image). The brain processes two things simultaneously = can't scroll past without pausing.

**Copy rules (apply to all ad copy, landing pages, and WhatsApp messages):**
- 5th grade reading level — same level as NYT bestsellers. Short sentences. 1–2 syllable words.
- Write to ONE person — "you / your." Never "investors, buyers, clients."
- Sell the click, not the property — the ad's job is curiosity + benefit, not a spec sheet
- Provide value first — insight, a finding, a number — before any pitch
- Gain frame beats fear frame 8/10 times — "earn 10% guaranteed" beats "don't miss out"
- Specificity = credibility: "10% annual yield, guaranteed in writing" beats "strong returns"
- Max 2,200 characters for Meta placements

**Hook Sources — Where Ad Ideas Come From**

Every ad and post pulls from the guide's (OLC's / Matthieu's) own material. Hook ideas come from:

- Epiphany bridge stories (the four in the story inventory)
- Frameworks being taught
- The red ocean being thrown rocks into (agencies, portals, fragmented listing)
- The new opportunity (outsourced performance sales)
- The cause and movement ("Stop Listing, Start Selling" / Market Makers tribe)
- The platform (OLC's methodology and track record)
- Identity shifts for the developer ("I am now someone who has a real sales system")
- Manifesto statements
- Milestone awards and client results
- Offers and offer elements
- Presentation excerpts

**The Three Most Profitable Ad Types (in order)**

1. **Epiphany Bridge Story ads** — primary format. A story about how you discovered the mechanism / created the lead magnet / built the new opportunity. Five phases below.
2. **SOAP opera email sequences** — the email funnel that runs after every opt-in. Breaks the four false beliefs over nine emails.
3. **Live presentations repurposed as ads** — Facebook Lives and webinar replays cut into paid ads.

**Ad Creative Framework — Epiphany Bridge Story (5 Phases)**

Every story ad follows this sequence. Expand or compress for format (Reel, static caption, WhatsApp opener, live video):

1. **Backstory** — where the developer was before (failed agents, stalled sales, cutting prices, frustrated)
2. **Journey** — what they tried that didn't work; villain is always a false belief or broken vehicle, never a person
3. **New opportunity** — the moment the old vehicle was abandoned and a different path appeared
4. **Framework** — the decision or mechanism that made the shift possible
5. **Achievement** — where they are now; stated plainly, no hype

Rules:
- Speak at plain language level — no yields, no percentages, no jargon in the hook
- Lead with the character and their desire, not the property
- The story is about the developer (the hero) — Matthieu is the guide
- One story per buyer profile (Pure Investor, Lifestyle Investor, Member) — match to the audience segment
- End with a CTA to the framework: "Click to get the free audit / access the framework"
- Reference: `expert-secrets-reference.md` Sections 7 and 15

**Short-Form Video Content — Facebook Lives & Reels (Pre-Webinar)**

The 5-minute script is the immediate-action format. It runs before the webinar is built and feeds paid ads directly. One well-executed Facebook Live becomes an always-on ad that drives cold traffic into the funnel.

Use the 8-question shortcut (Sections 13-14 of `expert-secrets-reference.md`) to prep any live in under 15 minutes:

1. What's the new opportunity I'm offering?
2. What's the offer stack for buyers?
3. What is the Big Domino? ("How to [result] with [vehicle]")
4. What's my origin story to knock down the Big Domino?
5. First secret — vehicle false belief, truth, story, title
6. Second secret — internal false belief, truth, story, title
7. Third secret — external false belief, truth, story, title
8. How do I structure the stack and close?

Ready-to-use script for OLC: `marketing/5min-script-olc.md`

Rules for Facebook Lives:
- Go live on a real account (Matthieu's personal or OLC page), not a produced video — authenticity converts better than polish at this stage
- Keep it 5 minutes maximum
- End with a specific CTA: "Message me 'PARTNER' to get the free audit"
- Repurpose as a paid ad immediately after it converts organically

**Diagnosis — if the presentation isn't converting:**
1. Wrong market — no one wants what you're offering
2. Improvement offer, not a new opportunity
3. Teaching tactics before selling the strategy (the vehicle)
Fix the market, the opportunity switch, or the belief-breaking sequence — in that order.

## Live Presentation Funnel (Warm Audience Lead Gen)

The webinar funnel converts warm contacts — LinkedIn connections, Bali developer network, conference contacts — into qualified discovery calls. It runs alongside cold outreach. One webinar that works consistently is worth more than months of daily cold DMs. Build this once the first client case study is complete.

**Trigger to build:** First close on record (Nirvana or equivalent). The case study is the non-negotiable input — without it, the Stack slide has no reference point and the Three Secrets have no third-party proof.

**Headline formula:** "How to [sell out your development project] without [paying agency retainers / cutting your price / waiting for slow agents]"

Applied: "How to sell out your Bali development project without paying a single retainer before you close."

**8-step sequence:**

1. **Traffic → registration page** — curiosity-first headline. One image that creates a question. Urgency built in (limited seats or specific run date).
2. **Thank-you page → SLO** — paid strategy session ($1,500–$2,000). Bridges the gap between the free audit and the $15K onboarding identified in `company/brand-foundation.md` Section 3. Developer pays to build their offer brief with Matthieu — pre-qualifies intent and offsets ad spend.
3. **Indoctrination emails (SOAP Opera Sequence)** — 9-email sequence that runs the moment someone opts in. Breaks all four false beliefs and delivers the full presentation over email before the live webinar. Structure: emails 1–5 are emotional (one story per email, Stack introduced in email 5), emails 6–7 are logic (ROI, proof, cost of inaction), emails 8–9 are fear (urgency, deadline, what they lose if they don't move). Every email ends with a hook that pulls them into the next one. False beliefs to break for OLC: vehicle ("sales partners don't work" — origin story), internal ("an outsider can't sell my specific project"), external ("the market is too slow right now"). See `expert-secrets-reference.md` Section 15 (SOAP Opera structure) and Section 8 (false belief framework).
4. **Reminders** — multi-touch leading up to the live date. Reinforce the Big Domino.
5. **Live presentation** — Big Domino → Three Secrets → Stack and Close. See `expert-secrets-reference.md` Section 9.
6. **Follow-up + urgency** — replay window with a real countdown. Not open-ended.
7. **Close the card** — hard cutoff. Replay access and special price removed simultaneously.
8. **Repeat** — each run produces data. Optimize the hook, tighten whichever secret loses the room, improve the Stack slide. One funnel, perfected over time.
