## Lead Gen Infrastructure

**Landing pages**
- Optimized for the offer designed in Phase 1
- One page per audience segment if targeting multiple buyer profiles
- Lead to a clear next action: application, site visit request, or info pack download

**Meta Ads (primary paid channel)**
- Targeting derived from the market identified in Phase 1A
- Ad budget is always the developer's cost — paid directly to Meta, not through OLC
- OLC manages the campaigns on the developer's behalf

**Ad Creative Framework — 32-Second Epiphany Bridge**

Every ad hook follows this structure. Expand or compress depending on format (story ad, reel, static image caption, whatsapp opener):

1. **Back story** — where the buyer was before (the pain, the frustration, the failed attempts)
2. **The journey** — what they tried, why it didn't work (the villain: wrong vehicle, wrong advisor, bad timing)
3. **New opportunity** — what changed; the moment they found a different path
4. **Framework** — the decision they made (the simple action that shifted everything)
5. **Achievement** — where they are now; the result, stated plainly

Rules:
- Speak at a plain level — no yields, no percentages, no jargon in the hook
- Lead with the character and their desire, not the property
- The villain is always a false belief or broken vehicle, not a person
- One story per buyer profile (Pure Investor, Lifestyle Investor, Member) — match the story to the audience segment being targeted
- Reference: `expert-secrets-reference.md` section 7 — 32-Second Epiphany Bridge Script

**Outreach automation sequences**
- Email, WhatsApp, and any active channel relevant to the audience
- Sequences built in Phase 1G (Sales Playbook) are deployed here
- CRM-triggered: lead enters pipeline → sequence starts automatically

## Lead Qualification

**CRM setup (GoHighLevel)**
- Pipeline stages configured based on Phase 1 ICP green lights and red flags
- Automated tagging and lead routing
- Every lead tracked from first touch to close

**Lead scoring**
- Score each new lead against the 6-factor ICP rubric from `company/icp.md`
- Score 13+: priority follow-up within 24 hours
- Score 9–12: active pipeline, standard sequence
- Below 9: low-priority or disqualify

## Live Presentation Funnel (Warm Audience Lead Gen)

The webinar funnel converts warm contacts — LinkedIn connections, Bali developer network, conference contacts — into qualified discovery calls. It runs alongside cold outreach. One webinar that works consistently is worth more than months of daily cold DMs. Build this once the first client case study is complete.

**Trigger to build:** First close on record (Nirvana or equivalent). The case study is the non-negotiable input — without it, the Stack slide has no reference point and the Three Secrets have no third-party proof.

**Headline formula:** "How to [sell out your development project] without [paying agency retainers / cutting your price / waiting for slow agents]"

Applied: "How to sell out your Bali development project without paying a single retainer before you close."

**8-step sequence:**

1. **Traffic → registration page** — curiosity-first headline. One image that creates a question. Urgency built in (limited seats or specific run date).
2. **Thank-you page → SLO** — paid strategy session ($1,500–$2,000). Bridges the gap between the free audit and the $15K onboarding identified in `company/brand-foundation.md` Section 3. Developer pays to build their offer brief with Matthieu — pre-qualifies intent and offsets ad spend.
3. **Indoctrination emails** — 3–5 emails breaking the three developer false beliefs before the webinar: "Sales partners don't work" (origin story), "An outsider can't sell my specific project" (internal belief), "The market is too slow right now" (external belief). See `expert-secrets-reference.md` Section 8.
4. **Reminders** — multi-touch leading up to the live date. Reinforce the Big Domino.
5. **Live presentation** — Big Domino → Three Secrets → Stack and Close. See `expert-secrets-reference.md` Section 9.
6. **Follow-up + urgency** — replay window with a real countdown. Not open-ended.
7. **Close the card** — hard cutoff. Replay access and special price removed simultaneously.
8. **Repeat** — each run produces data. Optimize the hook, tighten whichever secret loses the room, improve the Stack slide. One funnel, perfected over time.

**Status:** Not yet built. Queued after first close. Requires case study.
