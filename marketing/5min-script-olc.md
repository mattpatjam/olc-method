## The Script

"Hey, did you know that listing your development project with multiple agencies actually slows down your sales?

What is a performance sales partnership?

It's when one partner — not ten agents — owns the entire sales outcome for your project.

It's a done-for-you sales system: offer design, lead generation, and closing. You pay only when you close. No retainer, no upfront risk.

I'm going to share three secrets about this model and how you can fund your next project without paying a single retainer before you close.

But I'm going to show you exactly why that's a different model — and how the right approach gets very different results.

---

**The first secret is: The new opportunity isn't more agents — it's a different category.**

The big idea here is that agencies list your project and move on. A performance partner designs your offer, builds demand, and closes. They only win when you win.

This is important because listing with agencies creates a race to the bottom. Everyone cuts price. No one owns the result. A performance partner is the only relationship where your incentives are fully aligned.

---

**The second secret is: You don't need to be a better salesperson — you need one specialist who already is.**

The big idea here is that most developers close deals themselves or rely on agents who treat every property the same. A trained closer who understands concept value — not just price per square meter — is a different conversation entirely.

This is important because the gap between an amateur close and a trained one is often 20 to 30 percent of the deal — either lost on price, lost on hesitation, or lost because the buyer felt nothing.

---

**The third secret is: The market isn't slow — your offer is undifferentiated.**

The big idea here is that buyers in Bali right now aren't waiting for prices to drop. They're waiting for a project that makes the decision obvious. That's an offer design problem, not a market problem.

This is important because if your project looks like every other listing on the portal, it will perform like every other listing on the portal. The fix is in the offer, not the ad spend.

---

Now, I know what you're thinking.

It's: 'How do I know this will work for my specific project?'

The tricky thing is, most developers think their project is too unique to systematize — but that uniqueness is actually the asset we build the offer around. The more specific the concept, the more differentiated the offer, the easier the close.

And so I've built OLC Method — a done-for-you performance sales system — so you can launch a fully designed sales process without hiring a single full-time salesperson or paying a retainer before you close.

What makes this different: we design the offer, we build the demand, we close the deals. You approve everything. You only pay on results.

And so what this does is let you fund your next project faster — without being held hostage by agents who list and forget.

I, as a performance sales partner who has spent five years closing transactions across Bali and Lombok, what I do is take the offer design and closing system that works for top-performing projects and install it in yours.

I've built a process that gives you a complete sales machine — offer, leads, and closings — if you message me today.

You can message me the word PARTNER and I'll send you the details on the free audit — the first step where we map out what's holding your project back.

Message me PARTNER to get it today while it's still open. We're always here to help, and I can tell you about the results we've gotten for developers here in Bali who are dealing with exactly the same situation — great project, not enough qualified buyers closing.

I can't wait to see what we build together.

Have a great day."

## Delivery Notes

- Tone: direct, confident, conversational. Not a pitch — a conversation with one developer.
- No slides needed. Just face-to-camera.
- Say the CTA twice: once at the end, once pinned in the comments during the live.
- Repurpose immediately: download the replay, clip the best 60 seconds for Reels/Stories, run as a paid ad to cold developer audiences.

## The 8-Question Build (for adapting this script)

If you need to rebuild this for a specific project, audience, or platform, answer these eight questions first:

1. New opportunity: performance sales partnership (not an agency, not a retainer)
2. Offer stack: free audit → paid strategy session ($1,500–$2,000) → full engagement (onboarding fee + 5% performance)
3. Big Domino: "How to sell out your Bali development project without paying a single retainer before you close"
4. Origin story: five years as Senior Sales Manager, saw developers lose deals because agents couldn't communicate concept value, built a performance-only system
5. Vehicle false belief: "More agents = more sales" → truth: fragmented representation kills price and urgency
6. Internal false belief: "I can manage this myself" → truth: closing is a specialist skill; the gap costs 20-30% per deal
7. External false belief: "The market is too slow" → truth: undifferentiated offers perform slowly; the fix is offer design
8. Stack and close: free audit CTA → SLO (paid strategy session) → full engagement on performance terms
